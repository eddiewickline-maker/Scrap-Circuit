How to play

Moving

  • On a phone or tablet: ◀ ▶ walk, ▲ jumps. With Dig lit, hold on something to dig it or bonk a bot. With Build lit, tap to place, drag to lay wire, tap a switch to flip it, and hold on a part to pick it back up. Tap the hotbar to pick an item.
  • A D or arrows to walk, W or Space to jump
  • Hold left-click to dig, or to bonk a bot
  • Right-click to place the item you're holding. Hold and drag to lay wire.
  • Right-click a switch to flip it
  • 1–9 or the scroll wheel pick an item. C opens the workbench. M mutes. Esc pauses.

Circuits

  • A battery's + end is on its right, its − end on its left.
  • Electricity has to travel out of +, through your parts, and back into −. That full trip is a circuit, or loop.
  • A wire that dead-ends goes nowhere, so parts on it stay off.
  • Parts that touch are connected, even side by side. Leave a gap between wires that shouldn't join.
  • Wire straight from + to − with nothing in between is a short circuit. Nothing works, and in real life it gets dangerously hot.
  • Point at any part to see if it has power.

Gadgets

  • Transistor: a switch that electricity flips. Current goes through it left to right, but only while the wire touching its top or bottom has power.
  • Motion sensor: closes like a switch for a moment when something moves nearby.
  • Buzzer: beeps while powered. Battery + motion sensor + buzzer in a loop = burglar alarm.
  • Electromagnet: only a magnet while current flows. It pulls bots in and holds them stuck. Switch it off to let go.

Modes

  • Creative: unlimited parts, no bots, instant digging. Hold W or ▲ to fly up and S or ▼ to go down.
  • Simple electricity: anything touching a battery works. No + and − sides, no short circuits.
  • You pick both when you make a new world.

Scavenging

  • Old TVs, radios and laptops are full of parts. Dig them out.
  • Copper ore and scrap are deeper down. Take them to the workbench.
  • Bots won't appear near a lit lamp. Power doors keep them out of your base.

Save code

This page can't download files, so a save code is your backup. Copy it into a note or an email. To bring the world back, paste it in "Load a world from a save code" on the main menu, on any computer.
